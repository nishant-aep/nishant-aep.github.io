About me

Hi, I'm Nishant Mayekar, a video editor and a 12th-grade student. Editing started as a hobby, and it's now the thing I want to keep building on, because it clears my mind and lets me make something I'm proud of.

I'm studying commerce because I want to become a business accountant. Editing and accounting look very different, but both reward patience, attention to detail, and care for the small things nobody notices at first.

Thank you for taking the time to read this. If you like what you see, watch my edits on YouTube or say hi on Discord.

My editing journey

Alight Motion

This is where I started. I learned the basics of keyframes, timing, and transitions on my phone.

Preset era

I was a presetmaxxer for a while. It taught me what looks good, and later it pushed me to build my own style.

After Effects

I moved to After Effects and now edit on my own Acer Nitro V16S laptop, which gives me the power for heavier projects.

What I make

Movie edits
