About me

Hi, I'm Nishant Mayekar, a video editor and a 12th-grade student. Editing started as a hobby, and I want to keep building on it, because it clears my mind and lets me make something I'm proud of.

I started out on Alight Motion, and now I edit in Adobe After Effects on my own Acer Nitro V16S laptop. I make aesthetic edits and movie edits, and I used to be a presetmaxxer before I learned what After Effects is.

Outside of editing, my goal is to become a Twitch streamer. I want to build a community around it and keep editing alongside it.

Thank you for taking the time to read this. If you like what you see, watch my edits on YouTube or say hi on Discord.
